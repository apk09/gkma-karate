ABOUT GKMA


Goju-ryu, Japanese for "hard-soft style," is one of the main traditional Okinawan styles of karate, featuring a combination of hard and soft techniques.

Goju-Ryu Karate-Do Martial Arts Association (G.K.M.A) was founded by our President & Chief Technical Director Shihan Prem N. Khadka in 1996. GKMA headed by Shihan Prem N. Khadka was established in an attempt to spread the Art of Karate and enlighten this Art all around the globe.

GKMA has a strength of more than 200 black belts, more than 15 Dojo Instructors & 20 Qualified Referee's. GKMA has developed a lot and presently it has more than 1000 students training Goju-Ryu under GKMA.
